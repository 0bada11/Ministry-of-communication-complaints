# CHALLENGE 03
## Ministry of Communications
### Classifying and Routing Citizens' Complaints and Inquiries

---

### 1. Problem Description

Entities affiliated with the Ministry of Communications receive large numbers of citizen complaints and inquiries about the level of digital services, which may require going through many stages before reaching a resolution.

These complaints differ in nature and in which department is responsible for handling them. Dealing with a large volume of complaints leads to difficulty in organizing, classifying, and tracking their status.

### 2. Impact of the Problem

This can result in:

- Delayed handling of complaints due to mismatched routing.
- Complaints being directed to the wrong department.
- Difficulty tracking the status of a complaint.
- Difficulty prioritizing complaints.
- Duplicate complaints and suggestions.
- Difficulty extracting insights and evaluating the types of problems.

### 3. Why Does This Problem Need a Solution?

Communications and internet services are essential, high-usage services — response speed and effective handling and follow-up of citizens' issues matters greatly.

The growing volume of complaints and internet usage requires more capacity to track each complaint through more analytical steps, from the moment it's received until it's closed.

### 4. What Is Expected From the Solution?

The solution is expected to:

- Receive complaints and inquiries.
- Classify them.
- Determine their priority.
- Route them to the responsible department.
- Track their status.
- Provide a clear record for each complaint.
- Provide statistics about complaints.

---

### 5. Requirements

The MVP must include:

- Creating a complaint.
- Problem description.
- Complaint type.
- Attachments.
- Priority.
- Complaint status.
- Update log/history.
- Status and classification.
- Reference number.

Suggested statuses:

`New` . `Assigned` . `In Progress` . `Resolved` . `Closed`

`Dashboard`
